1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies can be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (remain on your device until they expire or are deleted).

In short: Cookies help us remember you, improve your experience, and understand how people use Huddle. We only use cookies that make sense for our platform.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: To make Huddle work properly (login, security, navigation).
  • Functional: To remember your preferences and settings.
  • Analytics: To understand how you use Huddle and improve our platform.
  • Marketing: To deliver relevant ads and measure campaign effectiveness.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for Huddle to function. Cannot be disabled.

📌 Examples: session_id, auth_token Essential ⏱ Session
Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences and settings.

📌 Examples: preferred_sport, location, theme Functional ⏱ 1 year
Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how you interact with Huddle.

📌 Examples: _ga, _gid (Google Analytics) Analytics ⏱ 2 years
Marketing Cookies

Deliver relevant ads and track campaign performance.

📌 Examples: _fbp, _gcl_au Marketing ⏱ 90 days

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that may place their own cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand traffic and user behavior.
  • Google Ads / Facebook Pixel: For marketing and retargeting.
  • Payment Processors: For secure payment processing.

These third parties have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

5. Manage Your Preferences

You can control how cookies are used on Huddle:

Essential Cookies Required for Huddle to function properly
Functional Cookies Remember your preferences and settings
Analytics Cookies Help us improve Huddle by understanding usage
Marketing Cookies Deliver relevant ads and promotions

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies

Please note that disabling cookies may affect some features of Huddle.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • Email notification.
  • A notice on our website.
  • Updating the "Last updated" date above.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
